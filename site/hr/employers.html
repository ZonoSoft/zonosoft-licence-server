Using ZonoSoft HR with your staff

ZonoSoft HR keeps your staff's information on your devices, not ours. That makes your business the one responsible for it under data protection law. This page explains what that involves and gives you a notice you can hand to your team.

General information, not legal advice. Last updated 28 November 2026.

What the app holds about your staff

  • Record: name, preferred name, staff ID and payroll number, role and job title, date of birth (for under-18 rules and minimum wage checks), contracted hours, availability, holiday allowance, email, address, phone numbers, emergency contacts, start date, probation dates, line manager and your notes.
  • Pay: pay rate, National Insurance number and bank details.
  • Working time: rotas, holiday, sickness, unpaid and family leave, training days, clock-in, break and clock-out times, and (if you turn on clocking by phone) where the person was at that moment.
  • HR records: documents you add (contracts, ID and right to work copies, fit notes, certificates, letters), sickness records including the reason and fit notes, family leave, right to work checks, starting and leaving details, reviews, and disciplinary and grievance cases.
  • Logins: username and a hashed password, and a log of sign-ins, changes and who looked at bank details or downloaded records.

It's stored encrypted on your devices and, if you share it between devices, in your own OneDrive, Google Drive or network folder. ZonoSoft never receives it. Team members' own phones only get their own shifts, clock times, holiday, their own contact and bank details, and their own requests. Sickness records, documents, reviews, cases and your notes never go to the Team app.

Health and other sensitive information

  • Sickness reasons and fit notes are health information, a "special category" under UK GDPR. You can keep them to meet your duties as an employer (for example sick pay and health and safety), but you need an appropriate policy document explaining how you handle this kind of information. The ICO's guidance on special category data includes a template.
  • Record only what you need: "flu" is enough; a diagnosis rarely is. The reason box is optional.
  • Keep disciplinary and grievance records factual. The person can ask for a copy, and the records may be seen at a tribunal.
  • ZonoSoft HR doesn't record criminal records checks.

Clock-in location

  • The phone's location is read only at the moment someone clocks in or out, to check they're within the distance of their work site you set. It isn't tracked at any other time.
  • For people marked "Works on the move", the location at clocking is recorded on the timesheet instead of checked.
  • Tell staff before you turn this on, and keep it proportionate. A short written assessment of why you need it (a data protection impact assessment) is good practice. See the ICO's guidance on monitoring workers.
  • Staff without a suitable phone can clock in on the shop's store terminal instead, which doesn't use location.

Fingerprint and face sign-in

Fingerprint, face and Windows Hello are checked by the phone or PC itself. Neither your business nor ZonoSoft ever receives the fingerprint or face, so you aren't collecting biometric data. It's optional for each person; they can always use their password.

Your main responsibilities

  • Tell your staff what you record and why (the template below covers the app).
  • Lawful basis: usually running the employment contract, legal obligations (working time, pay, holiday, sick pay and right to work records) and your legitimate interests (checking attendance, reviews). For health information, also the employment condition in the Data Protection Act 2018, with an appropriate policy document.
  • Keep it secure: give each person their own login, turn on two-step sign-in for managers, keep devices locked, and only show bank and NI details when you need them.
  • Keep it only as long as you need it: holiday and holiday pay records must be kept for 6 years; right to work check copies for 2 years after someone leaves. ZonoSoft HR shows each leaver's keep-until dates and, after 6 years, lets you remove their personal details while keeping the rota and holiday records.
  • Staff requests: if someone asks for a copy of their information, open their profile and choose Their data. Check it, remove other people's details, and reply within one month. Staff can ask for corrections from the Team app.
  • Check before you act: the app records and reminds; it doesn't give legal advice. For dismissals, long-term sickness or a difficult case, talk to ACAS or an employment adviser.

Template notice for your staff

Copy this into your staff handbook or noticeboard and fill in the parts in brackets.

How [Business name] uses your information in ZonoSoft HR

We use ZonoSoft HR to keep your employee record, plan rotas, book holiday, record when you start and finish work, and work out the hours we pay. It holds your name, staff ID, role, date of birth, contact details, emergency contacts, pay rate, National Insurance number and bank details, contracted hours, availability, holiday, sickness and other absence, and the times you clock in and out. It also holds documents and records such as your contract, right to work check, fit notes, reviews and, if there are any, disciplinary or grievance records.

Sickness information is health information. We keep only what we need to pay you correctly and support you, and only managers can see it.

[If clocking by phone is on:] When you clock in or out on your phone, the app checks your location at that moment to confirm you're at work. It doesn't track you at any other time. [For people who work on the move: where you were when you clocked in or out is recorded on your timesheet.]

If you choose to sign in with your fingerprint or face, that check happens only on your phone; we never receive it.

The information is stored securely on our devices [and in our OneDrive / Google Drive]. The app's maker, ZonoSoft, can't see it. On your own phone, the ZonoSoft HR Team app holds only your own shifts, clock times, holiday and details.

We use it to run your employment contract, meet our legal duties on working time, pay, holiday, sick pay and right to work, and check attendance. We keep holiday and pay records for 6 years and right to work checks for 2 years after you leave, and delete other records when we no longer need them.

You can see and ask to correct your details in the ZonoSoft HR Team app, or ask for a copy of all your information, by speaking to [name / role] or emailing [email]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
